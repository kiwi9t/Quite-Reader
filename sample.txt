Chapter 1: The Lantern Room

Mira found the old reading room just before sunset. The windows were tall, the floorboards were quiet, and a brass lantern waited on the desk as if someone had left it there only a moment ago.

She opened her notebook and copied the first sentence from the book in front of her. Some words felt familiar, but others seemed to hide behind the page. Mira did not want the ending explained. She only wanted enough help to keep reading.

Outside, the town bell rang six times. Inside, the lantern gave a small golden flicker.

Chapter 2: A Map Without Names

The next page showed a map with rivers, hills, and three empty circles where city names should have been. Mira traced one river with her finger and noticed a tiny note in the corner: Begin where the road forgets itself.

She smiled at the strange sentence. It was not clear yet, but it was interesting enough to continue.
